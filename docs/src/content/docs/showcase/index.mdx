Here's a real work example of the kit in action. A bunch of components on one page, the way you'd actually use them.

## Member profile

Maya Chen's account. A card holds who she is. [Tabs](/components/tabs/) hold the rest.

Points is a [gauge](/components/gauge/) at 2,450 of 3,000 toward the next $25 credit. Deals is a [carousel](/components/carousel/) of three offers. Activity is a [table](/components/table/) of what she's done. Show on a row opens that entry in a [modal](/components/modal/).

The panels need real markup, so the tabs take their content through the `panels` property, not the JSON attribute. Same move as [How it works](/how-it-works/). The dialogs sit beside the tabs, not inside a panel. A hidden panel can swallow a popover.
